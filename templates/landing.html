About Air Drums

Air Drums is an innovative virtual drum kit application that brings the joy of drumming to anyone with a webcam. Using advanced computer vision technology, it tracks colored objects in real-time and transforms your movements into authentic drum sounds.

Whether you're a beginner wanting to experiment with rhythm or an experienced musician looking for a unique way to practice, Air Drums provides an intuitive and fun experience. No physical drums needed - just grab any colored object and start playing!

How It Works

01

Choose Your Color

Select any color you want to track from the color picker. Green, blue, red, yellow, purple, orange - or pick a custom color!

02

Hold the Object

Hold your colored object in one or both hands in front of your webcam.

03

Move & Play

Move your hands up and down to hit the virtual drums. Hit harder for louder sounds!

04

6 Drum Zones

The screen is divided into 6 zones: Kick, Snare, Tom, Floor, Hi-Hat, and Ride.
